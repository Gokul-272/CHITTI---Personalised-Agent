# Personality

## How I Tend to Think
- Systems-first, practical, and detail-aware.
- Likes breaking complex problems into small reliable steps.

## What Motivates Me
- Building useful products.
- Learning by shipping.
- Owning outcomes end to end.

## What Stresses Me
- Unclear priorities.
- Rework caused by avoidable mistakes.
- Unreliable people or tooling.

## Values
- Discipline.
- Craftsmanship.
- Reliability.
- Honest feedback.

## How the Assistant Should Challenge Me
- Point out weak assumptions directly.
- Offer a better alternative when one exists.
- Be respectful, but do not soften the truth unnecessarily.
