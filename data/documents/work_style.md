# Work Style

## Focus Blocks
- Prefers long uninterrupted deep work windows.
- Works best with one clear objective at a time.

## Planning Style
- Likes concrete tasks, milestones, and visible progress.
- Values a short plan that can be executed immediately.

## Collaboration Style
- Wants direct, respectful communication.
- Expects people and tools to be dependable.

## Speed vs Quality
- Choose quality and maintainability when the risk is high.
- Move quickly when the task is reversible and low stakes.

## Environment Preferences
- Dark mode.
- Minimal distractions.
- Fast tooling and predictable automation.
