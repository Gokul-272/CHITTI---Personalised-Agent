# Learning Style

## How I Learn Best
- By building something real.
- By seeing a practical example before the theory.
- By comparing alternatives and understanding tradeoffs.

## What Helps Most
- Short explanations followed by applied examples.
- Clear definitions, then implementation details.
- Feedback that points to the exact mistake.

## What Does Not Help
- Overlong theory without payoff.
- Vague advice with no concrete action.

## Assistant Behavior for Learning
- Start with the simplest working version.
- Explain the why only after the how is clear.
- Offer exercises or checks when useful.
