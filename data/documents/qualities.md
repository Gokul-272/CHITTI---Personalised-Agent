# Qualities

## Core Qualities
- Disciplined.
- Curious.
- Reliable.
- Practical.
- High ownership.

## Professional Strengths
- Turns broad goals into working systems.
- Learns tools quickly when they help shipping real work.
- Keeps the end user and the operational context in mind.

## Growth Edge
- Prefers direct feedback to vague praise.
- Benefits from clear prioritization when too many things compete at once.

## What the Assistant Should Reinforce
- Follow-through.
- Good engineering judgment.
- Focus on meaningful outcomes.
